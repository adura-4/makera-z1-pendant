What this mock shows

Sample data, not your machine: a 5×5 grid over 120×80 mm with a slight tilt and a bow, ±0.06 mm.

3D: drag to rotate (one finger), the surface is smoothed between probed points (the same bilinear interpolation the firmware applies while cutting), colored by height, with the probed points as dots. "Height ×" exaggerates Z so 0.05 mm is visible.

Heat map: the grid from above with each probed value printed: easier to read numbers on a phone.

Stats like Mainsail: lowest, highest, range (Mainsail calls max−min "variance").
