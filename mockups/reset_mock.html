Reset asks first, and says what it does
Same frame as the spindle boxes (✕ top-right, one button at the bottom), in red. Hold stays a single tap: it's the quick way to pause. Wording on the right is shorter; pick one.
A · Explained
warningReset machine?
close
Stops everything at once, like an emergency stop:
  • motion and the spindle stop
  • a running job is cancelled (it can't be resumed)
  • the controller halts: Unlock before moving again
To pause and carry on, use Hold instead.
Ctrl-X (0x18)
