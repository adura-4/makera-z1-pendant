Pendant: spindle temperature

The Z1 already sends the spindle temperature in every status (|S: 5th number) and the electronics box's (6th number). Shown only while a job runs: a graph in the job panel, a warning before the Z1's own limit, and alarm sheets that say which part stopped and why. Nothing changes in the top bar or while idle. Numbers from today's overheat (36 °C at the start, 75.1 °C about 2½ min into the cut).  dashed orange  = new.

1. During a job

The job panel gets a Temperature card graphing both since the job started: spindle in blue (its 75 °C limit dashed red), box in violet (its 60 °C limit dotted). Each has its own scale, with tick numbers in its colour. Limits are read from the machine. Tool marks along the bottom: the job's first tool at the start, then a dot and a faint line where each next tool started, so you can see what each tool did to the temperature.

2. Getting close

At 5 °C under the limit (70 °C here): a banner, one buzz, and the spindle line pulses (orange, glowing in and out about once a second; the rest of the graph stays as is). Box's line does the same near its 60 °C. The pendant doesn't stop anything itself; you can Hold or turn the spindle/feed down. Gone again when it drops back under.

4. The driver board stops it

"Spindle alarm triggered" is the driver board's own fault line, a different part. The sheet says so, what it can mean, and the temperatures at that moment, so you can tell the two apart later.

Where the numbers come from (checked)

  • Spindle temperature: |S: 5th number, from the thermistor on the motion board's TEMP-SPD connector (P1.31). Box: 6th number (P1.30).
  • The limit: config-get sd temperature_control.spindle.max_temp (your SD config says 75; the firmware default is 70). Box limit 60.
  • "Spindle overheated, max …, current …" and "Spindle alarm triggered – power off/on required" are the two messages from today; the pendant would match them to pick sheet 3 or 4.
  • There's no temperature from the driver board itself; the Z1 never gets one.

Not shown (on purpose)

  • No automatic Hold or slow-down by the pendant: it warns, you decide.
  • No separate history screen: the graph covers the current job, and the last job's graph stays in Job details until the next one starts.

Defaults I picked (say if you want them different)

  1. Warning at limit − 5 °C (70 °C on yours), one buzz; amber from limit − 15 °C (60 °C).
  2. Nothing in the top bar and nothing while idle; the temperatures appear with the job panel.
  3. Own scale per line (spindle 20–80 °C, box 20–65 °C) so both limits sit near the top; no legend, the colours say which is which.
  4. Tool marks show the tool number only (T8, T1…); the tool names are in the tools card just above. A mark goes where the tool change finished ("Done ATC"), so the spindle-off time of the change sits just before it.
  5. Clear alarm after an overheat waits for under-the-limit (greyed with the live reading).
  6. The ↑ °C/min rate shown next to the reading.
